Precision PC Craftsmanship at Every Stage

Unlike mass assemblers, BuildFlow coordinates every custom computer through our proprietary state machine: Assembly Task → QA Inspection → Insured Logistics.

01.

Automated Spec & TDP Cross-Validation

Our system computes physical clearances (GPU length, cooler radiator clearance, RAM height), socket compatibility, and 12V rail wattage headroom before picking components.

02.

ESD-Protected Hand Assembly

Certified technicians assemble your hardware with calibrated torque drivers, apply premium thermal paste using a stencil matrix, and lace cables behind backplates.

03.

24-Hour Multi-Stress QA Benchmarking

MemTest86 memory soak, Cinebench multicore sustained thermal load, and 3DMark TimeSpy extreme loops ensure zero throttling and BIOS stability.

04.

Expanding Foam Shock-Packaging

High-density expanding InstaPak foam molds directly around your heavy GPU and CPU cooler inside the chassis, preventing transit damage before express insured shipment.
